Once I had more than three or four long-running terminal sessions going at once, keeping track of which one was doing what became its own chore. Session names defaulted to numbers, windows all looked the same at a glance, and I'd regularly kill the wrong one.

So I wrote a small wrapper script that names sessions after the project and task they're tied to, shows a quick status line for each, and lets me jump straight to one by fuzzy-matching a few letters instead of remembering an index.

## The part that mattered most

Auto-naming turned out to matter more than any of the fancier features I added later. The moment sessions had meaningful names, the "which one was that in?" problem mostly disappeared on its own.

It's a small utility — a few hundred lines, nothing clever — but it's one of those tools that quietly saves a few seconds dozens of times a day, and that adds up.
