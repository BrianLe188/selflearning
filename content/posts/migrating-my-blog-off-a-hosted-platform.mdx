My blog spent its first two years on a hosted platform, mostly because it let me start writing without thinking about infrastructure at all. That trade-off made sense at the time, and I don't regret it — but it eventually started to chafe.

Custom layouts fought the platform's constraints, exporting my own content felt harder than it should have been, and I wanted the writing itself to live somewhere I fully controlled.

## What I gained

Full control over the markup, the styling, and exactly how content is stored — plain files in a folder, readable without any special tool. Deploys are simple, and there's no platform-specific lock-in to worry about anymore.

## What I lost

Comments, built-in analytics, and the zero-setup convenience of just writing and hitting publish. I've rebuilt small pieces of that myself, and it's been worth it, but it wasn't free.
