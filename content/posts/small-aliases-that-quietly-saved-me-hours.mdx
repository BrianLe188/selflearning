None of these are clever. That's kind of the point — they're the boring, repetitive commands I was typing dozens of times a day, shortened down to a few characters each.

## The aliases

A short one for jumping back to a project's root no matter how deep I am in its folder structure. Another for the exact git log format I always want but never remember the flags for. A third that runs my whole local test-and-lint sequence in one word instead of three separate commands typed in the right order.

## Why they add up

Each one saves a few seconds. None of them feel significant in the moment. But they're commands I run dozens of times a day, and a year of those seconds adds up to something that actually shows up in how much friction the day has — which, it turns out, is most of what these kinds of small habits are about.
