I'd been meaning to learn these for years and kept reaching for the mouse instead, because in the moment it always felt faster. It wasn't — it just felt that way because the cost was spread across hundreds of tiny interruptions instead of one visible chunk of time.

## The three that stuck

Jumping between split panes without touching the trackpad. Fuzzy-finding a file by a few letters instead of clicking through a tree. And a single shortcut for "stage this, commit it, and move on" that collapsed a three-step ritual into one keystroke.

None of these are exotic. What made them stick this time wasn't a cheat sheet — it was forcing myself to use them for two full weeks even when the old way felt faster, until the new way actually was.
