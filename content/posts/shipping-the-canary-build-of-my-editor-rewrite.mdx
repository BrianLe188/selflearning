After months of work behind a feature flag, the rewritten editor core shipped to a small canary group this week. Nothing catastrophic happened, which honestly felt like the biggest win of the release.

The parts I'm proud of: startup time is down significantly, and the plugin API is finally stable enough that I trust it not to break on the next release. Early feedback on both has been better than I expected.

## What I'm still unsure about

The new undo model is technically more correct, but a couple of canary users have said it "feels different" in ways they can't quite articulate. I don't have a fix yet — just a note to pay close attention to that feedback before a wider rollout.

Shipping something you're not 100% sure about is uncomfortable, but a canary group exists precisely so you can find that out before everyone else does.
