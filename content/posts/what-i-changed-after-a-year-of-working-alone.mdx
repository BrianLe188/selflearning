A year into working solo, some of the routines I started with turned out to matter a lot, and a few others I quietly abandoned once I was honest about whether they were actually helping.

## What stuck

A real, fixed start to the workday — same time, same first task, no exceptions. Regular calls with other people who work alone too, just to talk through problems out loud instead of only in my own head. And a hard stop at the end of the day that I treat as non-negotiable as a meeting I couldn't miss.

## What I let go of

The idea that I needed a rigid schedule for every hour of the day. Structure helped with the big transitions — starting and stopping — but forcing every block of time into a plan just meant constantly feeling behind. Loosening that up, without losing the parts that actually worked, made the whole year easier than the first few months were.
